# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy and focus criticism on technical work. Handle security reports privately.

This project monitors real people's accounts. Do not use issues, discussions or pull requests to publish someone's Instagram data, to seek help targeting a specific person or to share credentials belonging to anyone.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Using the project's spaces to coordinate stalking or surveillance of an individual is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at <misiektoja-github@rm-rf.ninja>. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately and respectful of reporter confidentiality.
